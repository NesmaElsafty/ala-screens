Dr.Ahmed Latif

Who He Is

Born on September 25, 1988, in Cairo, Egypt, Dr. Ahmed Latif Mohamed Hassan has built a remarkable career spanning corporate leadership, academic excellence, and large-scale public training. He holds a PhD in Business Administration from Docklands Academy London, an MBA from Docklands Academy London, and a B.Sc. in Management Information Systems from Al-Madina Academy, Al-Haram.

His professional journey includes leadership at United Company of Pharmacists (UCP) as Team Leader for Helwan & Embaba Branches, Marketing Manager at Logic Advertising Agency, and Supervisor at Tiens Tianshi Corp — a multinational Chinese giant with 190 branches in 110 countries — where he was responsible for 900 individuals and selected by the Chinese Board of Directors to train 3,000 employees across Egypt.

He founded Ahmed Latif Academy (ALA) for Training and Consultancies, delivering a distinctive methodology that draws from Chinese, Indian, American, and European schools of thought — refined through years of practical application and interactive training.

Dr. Latif is an ICF Professional Certified Coach (PCC) with over 6,000 hours of coaching experience. He is a special coach for VIP clients in business, entrepreneurship, and public life, combining academic depth, corporate experience, and genuine human connection in every engagement.

His Philosophy

"Man has gigantic energies that enable him to change the world — as soon as he discovers them and directs them in the right direction." Every human being possesses immense potential that, once discovered and directed correctly, can transform lives and communities.

His training approach combines deep psychological insight with practical, interactive applications designed to produce real, measurable change. He integrates principles from positive psychology, self-persuasion, belief systems, and emotional intelligence into every program.

As a special coach for VIP clients in business, entrepreneurship, and public life, Dr. Latif brings a rare combination of academic depth, corporate experience, and genuine human connection to every engagement — whether addressing 850 students or coaching a single executive.
